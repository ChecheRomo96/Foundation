Linux generators are usually single-configuration generators. In this case, CMAKE_BUILD_TYPE is valid.

Example:

cmake --preset linux_gcc_x64_release
cmake --build --preset linux_gcc_x64_release
cmake --install build/linux_gcc_x64_release --prefix dist/linux_gcc_x64_release
