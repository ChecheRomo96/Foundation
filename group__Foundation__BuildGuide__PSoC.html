Why not every chip

Foundation should not initially generate one distribution per PSoC chip.

The minimum useful split is:

  • toolchain,
  • CPU core,
  • ABI/FPU mode,
  • configuration.

Since Foundation is mostly portable C++ and does not depend on chip registers, a PSoC 5LP Cortex-M3 soft-float build can cover the typical CY8CKIT-059 use case.
